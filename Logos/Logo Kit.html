Usage notes

keep it legible
Clear space

Leave at least the height of the "R" of breathing room on all sides. Don't crowd it.

Minimum size
24 px — too small
32 px — minimum

Below 32 px tall on screen, the "MALL" sub-label and birds start to disappear. Use the favicon files at very small sizes instead.
