# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Shoppers planning a visit (locals and visitors checking stores, hours, and directions) and prospective tenants/businesses considering leasing space. Both audiences arrive with a purpose — shoppers need to orient, businesses need to be convinced.

## Product Purpose

Rupa's Mall is Eldoret's premier shopping destination. The website is the mall's digital identity — it should inspire confidence, communicate premium, and help visitors navigate 45+ stores across 3 floors.

## Brand Personality

Refined, editorial, aspirational. The brand voice is confident and warm without being loud. Three words: curated, grounded, elevated.

## Anti-references

Generic mall directory layouts — flat white lists with icons and filters that feel like a 2012 mall website. The store cards should feel editorial and considered, not transactional.

## Design Principles

1. Every surface earns its premium — nothing looks free or default.
2. Let imagery do the heavy lifting — strong photos with restrained typography.
3. Warmth through specificity — gold and brown palette feels rooted in place, not globally generic.
4. Hierarchy over decoration — when in doubt, scale and weight over ornament.
5. Trust the brand system — Cormorant Garamond, Josefin Sans, and gold are non-negotiable anchors.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA minimum. Sufficient contrast on overlay text, readable at small sizes.
